# Research context

**Benchmarks and operating conditions.** Sommer and Paxson, *Outside the Closed World: On Using Machine Learning for Network Intrusion Detection* (IEEE S&P, 2010), describe why success on a closed benchmark may fail to transfer to operational monitoring. Here, per-benign false alarms and transfer to another collection complement within-corpus ranking. Saito and Rehmsmeier (PLOS ONE, 2015) explain why precision-recall analysis matters for imbalanced data. Our reported average precision and trapezoidal PR area use different integration conventions and can diverge. [Sommer and Paxson](https://oaklandsok.github.io/papers/sommer2010.pdf) · [Saito and Rehmsmeier](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0118432)

**Representations and provenance.** Moustafa and Slay introduced UNSW-NB15 at MilCIS 2015. Aggregating its traffic into NetFlow yields a different representation. Sarhan, Layeghy and Portmann's *Towards a Standard Feature Set for Network Intrusion Detection System Datasets* motivates comparing compatible measurements from multiple collections. A shared field dictionary does not equate their host populations or class frequencies. See the dataset audit for acquisition details and the full UNSW acknowledgment. [UNSW](https://research.unsw.edu.au/projects/unsw-nb15-dataset) · [Sarhan et al.](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11036-021-01843-0)

**Probability and review.** Guo, Pleiss, Sun and Weinberger, *On Calibration of Modern Neural Networks* (ICML 2017), studied a one-parameter rescaling approach. Geifman and El-Yaniv, *Selective Classification for Deep Neural Networks* (NeurIPS 2017), connect the reject option with error among accepted cases. SENTINEL-X fits maps on a development partition and measures realized test coverage. It provides no finite-sample guarantee under covariate shift. [Guo et al.](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/guo17a.html) · [Geifman and El-Yaniv](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2017/hash/4a8423d5e91fda00bb7e46540e2b0cf1-Abstract.html)

**Behavioural modelling.** Verwer and Hammerschmidt, *FlexFringe: Modeling software behavior by learning probabilistic automata* (Logical Methods in Computer Science 21(3), 2025), study state merging from observations. Our estimator assigns observable call tokens to states and estimates smoothed first-order transitions. Its edge likelihoods can be inspected directly. It does not learn merged latent states, and no result here compares it empirically with FlexFringe. The ADFA-LD archive supplies actual recorded syscall order. [Verwer and Hammerschmidt](https://doi.org/10.46298/lmcs-21(3:31)2025) · [ADFA data](https://research.unsw.edu.au/projects/adfa-ids-datasets)

**Evidence quality.** Gao, Yen, Yu and Chen, *Enabling Large Language Models to Generate Text with Citations* (EMNLP 2023), treated answer correctness and citation quality as distinct dimensions. We use that distinction for a more constrained task. A deterministic field statement may remain true when its citation is removed. Conversely, a link to a real event can point to the wrong case even if a common metadata field happens to match. Our review rubric checks event identity, relevant fields and missing evidence. [Gao et al.](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.398/)
